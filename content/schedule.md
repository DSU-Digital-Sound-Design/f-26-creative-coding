Class meets Mondays from 1:00 to 1:50 p.m. Bring a laptop, wired headphones,
and your current work. D2L has the current room and any meeting changes.

This is a working plan. Topics may change as we develop the ensemble and
individual projects. All deadlines are Central. Week numbers follow the
semester calendar beginning August 24; our first meeting was August 31.

## 10/5

- Develop a visual idea for [Creative Study 2](/projects/project-2/) and practice moving between Hydra states by editing and running the code.
### homework
- Develop a rough 60–90 second Hydra visual study with two or three states using the techniques practiced in class. Save the code and choose someone to give feedback before the deadline.
