Each week's etudes are short studies in writing musical patterns. To run an
example, click **load editor**, then **play**. Click **stop** to stop it.
You can also press **Ctrl+Enter** to play and **Ctrl+.** to stop.

Edit the code while it plays, then click **play** or press **Ctrl+Enter**
again to hear your changes.

Only one etude plays at a time. Loading a new editor stops whichever one was
running.

## How these studies fit the course

Save useful variations, screenshots or recordings, and notes about what changed.
You will select this evidence for the [practice checkpoints](/projects/).
The studies also give you starting points for the two creative studies.
