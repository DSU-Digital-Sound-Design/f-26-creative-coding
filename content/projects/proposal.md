Category: **Project Proposal and Milestones**.

**Due:** October 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM Central

Use the October 19 sharing and discussion, your creative studies, and your other
experiments to propose a manageable final ensemble role or independent project.
This proposal includes the direction-setting work previously listed as Practice
Checkpoint 2. There is one submission and one grade.

The October 26 [proposal workshop](/etudes/week-09/) gives you class time to
test an uncertainty, get feedback, and draft these sections. Use that evidence
to explain your choices; the workshop is part of developing this proposal.

## What can you propose?

An independent project can explore **anything that combines code and creativity**.
You can work with generative art, music, animation, experimental games, Arduino
and other microcontrollers, sensors, interactive objects, programmable DMX
lighting, movement, or installations. These are starting points; bring other
ideas that interest you. You can propose tools beyond Strudel and Hydra.

Explain what the code contributes to the creative experience. Choose a scope
that fits the time, equipment, and skills you can develop during the semester.
For a physical project, identify the components, interfaces, and space you will
need, what you already have access to, and what you still need to arrange.

## Find inspiration

- [CreativeApplications.Net](https://www.creativeapplications.net/) features work connecting art and technology, including sound, light, physical installations, and interactive objects.
- [Hello, p5.js!](https://hello.p5js.org/) is an interactive introduction with demonstrations of animation, audio, and interaction.
- The [p5.js examples collection](https://p5js.org/examples/) has small projects you can run and inspect, including drawing tools, generative poetry, games, and simulations.

You do not have to use p5.js. Screen-based examples and physical projects can
both suggest an approach. Identify one idea or technique you could explore
at a manageable scale, and explain how you would develop it in your own work.
Use the original artist's or developer's page when available, and credit any
code or other material you adapt. Your two proposal references can be artistic
works, performances, tutorials, or documented techniques.

## Submit

- A focused creative or research question
- Ensemble role or independent-project pathway
- Selected tools, planned output, and project boundaries, including equipment and space needs where relevant
- Links to a few existing experiments or studies that informed your direction, with a brief explanation of what you learned and how it shaped this proposal. Link to work already submitted rather than resubmitting it.
- Two relevant artistic or technical references
- A short milestone plan, major risks, and a fallback version

## Grading

This assignment is worth 20 points within its weighted category.

- Question and scope: 6 points
- Tool and pathway fit, supported by your experiments: 4 points
- Milestones and feasibility: 6 points
- References, risks, and fallback plan: 4 points
