Category: **Creative-Coding Studies**.

**Due:** October 16, 2026 at 11:59 PM Central

Make a **60–90 second visual study in Hydra** with one clear visual idea.
Start with the October 5 [First visuals études](/etudes/week-06/).

## What to develop

- Build two or three contrasting states and perform deliberate transitions
  between them by changing and running the code.
- Make choices about motion, density, color, or shape that support your idea.
  Be ready to explain which parameters create the differences.
- Show a rough version to someone, ask how the states differ in character,
  and revise one part in response.

Use the techniques practiced on October 5: sources such as `osc()` and
`shape()`, changes to their parameters, color and rotation, and simple blending
or repetition. Choose only the techniques that support your idea. You do not
need additional tools or functions to earn full credit.

To move between states, change values and run the code again, just as in the
études. Keep a saved copy of each state so you can repeat the sequence.
This is a visual study; sound is not required.

## Independent work and critique

There is no class meeting on **October 12, Native American Day**. Use the gap
between October 5 and the deadline to develop, test, and record your study.
We will share the finished work and discuss project directions in class on
**October 19**.

## Submit

- Your Hydra code, including the saved states, and a short note explaining how to run them in order.
- A 60–90 second screen capture showing your visual states and the changes between them.
- A 250 to 400 word note describing your visual idea, the parameters you changed, the feedback you received, and one revision you made
- Credits and links for code, media, tutorials, or AI assistance used

- Evidence of iteration and response to testing: 10 points
