## Course description

Creative Coding is a hands-on course in writing music as code. Students work in
Strudel, a browser-based live coding environment, building patterns from a
small notation and extending them week by week. Class sessions combine guided
etudes, in-class labs, and listening critique.

## What you need

Nothing to buy and nothing to install. A browser and a pair of headphones.
Everything runs at [strudel.cc](https://strudel.cc/), and your work is plain
text you can save anywhere.

## How the course runs

Each week has a set of [etudes](/etudes/): short studies built on a
deliberately narrow vocabulary. We work through them together in class, then
you extend one on your own. [Projects](/projects/) are longer pieces that pull
several weeks of technique into something you play for the room.

Everything you make gets played out loud. Be ready to share work in progress.

## Grading

| | |
|---|---|
| Projects | 50% |
| Weekly etudes | 30% |
| Attendance and participation | 20% |

## Attendance

The course meets once a week, so a single absence is a full week of material.
Come to class. If you have to miss, the etude page for that week has
everything we did, and every example on it runs in the page.

## Academic integrity

Live coding has a strong culture of sharing patterns and reading each other's
code. That is encouraged here. What matters is that you say where something
came from: if you built on a classmate's pattern or one you found online, name
the source in your submission. Passing someone else's work off as your own is
the only version of this that is a problem.
