Creative Coding is a studio course in making music, sound, visuals, and
performances with code. We meet once a week to try ideas, share unfinished
work, and decide what to test next. Between meetings, you develop your work
and keep a record of what changes.

We are starting with [Strudel](https://strudel.cc/), which runs in a browser.
The interactive [etudes](/etudes/) let you edit a pattern, play it, and hear
what your choices do. Save your experiments and notes for the
[practice checkpoints](/projects/).

Everyone begins by exploring the laptop-ensemble model. After two creative
studies, you will confirm an ensemble role or propose an independent project
for instructor approval. The semester ends with a performance or project
presentation and documentation that someone else can use to run your work.

## Our weekly meeting

- Share progress and current problems, about 5 minutes.
- Discuss one concept or artist example, about 10 minutes.
- Work through a live demonstration, about 15 minutes.
- Code, rehearse, troubleshoot, or critique, about 15 minutes.
- Record your next experiment, about 5 minutes.

Bring a laptop, wired headphones, and something to show, even if it is
unfinished or broken. No prior live-coding or electronic-music experience is
required. The course prerequisite is CSC 150.

Use the [schedule](/schedule/) to plan your work and the
[assignment briefs](/projects/) to check what to submit. D2L is the official
source for meeting changes, submissions, feedback, deadlines, and grades.
