Creative Coding is a course in writing music as code, live. You type a pattern,
press two keys, and hear it immediately. Then you change it while it is still
playing.

We work in [Strudel](https://strudel.cc/), which runs in a browser tab. There
is no software to install, no license to buy, and nothing that only works in
one lab. The pattern you write in class is a few lines of text you can paste
into a message, hand to the person next to you, or open again on any machine.

The weekly [etudes](/etudes/) are the spine of the course. Each one is a short
study built on a deliberately small vocabulary, the way a piano etude drills
one thing at a time. Most of them take away more than they give you: use only
two drum sounds, or make five of your eight slots silent, or change nothing
but the drum machine. Constraint is the teaching tool. It is much easier to be
inventive in a small space than an open one.

Class sessions are hands-on. We work through the etudes together, play the
results out loud, and describe what we hear before we say whether we like it.
