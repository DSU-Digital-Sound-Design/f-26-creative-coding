We are starting with Strudel. Other tools may suit later projects; choose them according to your interests and what the project needs. Browser-based tools keep setup simple.

## Music and sound

- [Strudel](https://strudel.cc/), browser-based pattern coding
- [Sonic Pi](https://sonic-pi.net/), approachable live coding for music and sound
- [Gibber](https://gibber.cc/), browser-based music, visuals, and ensemble networking
- [SuperCollider](https://supercollider.github.io/), synthesis and algorithmic composition
- [TidalCycles](https://tidalcycles.org/), pattern-based live coding

## Visuals

- [Hydra](https://hydra.ojack.xyz/), browser-based visual and video synthesis
- [Gibber](https://gibber.cc/), combined sound and visual coding
